# Credits

Choir Rehearsals characters by Kinari (kinari.church), CC BY 4.0.

The models were made in Blender, from code, by AI agents working under the direction of the author. No external assets were used: no downloaded models, textures, HDRIs, scans, fonts, brushes or generated images.

| Part | How it was made |
|---|---|
| Geometry (carved heads, jaw, eyes, lids, clothes, hands, boots and clogs, wind-up key) | Procedural mesh code, deterministic (fixed seed) |
| Paint and wear (polychrome, worn wood, patches, darns, seams, scarf stripes) | Vertex colours written by the same code |
| Relief (gouge marks, wood grain, weave) | Blender procedural nodes |
| Game textures (colour, normal, eye emission) | Baked in Blender (Cycles) from the film versions of the characters |
| Rig and 9 poses | Authored in code, exported as glTF animations |

Tools: Blender (GPL; files exported from it are not covered by the GPL) and the Khronos glTF I/O exporter.
